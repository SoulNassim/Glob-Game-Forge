# Chronicles of Aethelgard — Game Design Document (GDD)

Chronicles of Aethelgard is a stylized hand-painted low-poly isometric Action RPG set in ancient clockwork dungeon ruins infused with arcane alchemy.

## Visual Pillars
- **Art Style**: Hand-painted low-poly fantasy with crisp chunky silhouettes and warm golden-hour rim lighting.
- **Camera**: Isometric 3/4 orthographic 3D perspective.
- **Scale**: 1 Godot unit = 1 meter. Playable heroes stand 1.8m tall.
- **Lore Keywords**: arcane, clockwork, alchemy, ruins, crystal.
